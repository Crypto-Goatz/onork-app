// THE VPIS FORMULA

Not vibes.
A formula.

POST_SCORE = (Hook × 0.20) + (EmotionalTrigger × 0.18) + (PlatformOpt × 0.15) + (ViralCoeff × 0.13) + (Specificity × 0.12) + (Structure × 0.10) + (Keywords × 0.07) + (CTA × 0.05)
Hook
20%
First line forces a position. Pattern interrupt. No weak openers.
Emotional Trigger
18%
Career anxiety, FOMO, identity threat, financial aspiration.
Platform Opt
15%
No link in body. Zero hashtags. Native format. Algorithm-clean.
Viral Coefficient
13%
Save intent. Reshare mechanic. Quotable standalone lines.
Specificity
12%
Exact numbers. Named tools. Verifiable claims. No vagueness.
Structure
10%
Vibe Style. Short sentences. Breathing room. No corporate words.
Keywords
7%
ICP-matched vocabulary. Audience-specific signal density.
CTA
5%
Specific practitioner question. Answerable in 30 seconds.
