# 0nCore — AI Business Operating System

## Short Description (150 chars)
AI-powered business automation platform. 1,554 tools, 96 services, 15 AI knowledge layers. Add it one place — have it everywhere.

## Long Description

### Stop switching tabs. Start running your business.

0nCore is an AI business operating system that connects to your CRM and turns it into the command center for your entire operation. Every tool you need — social media, email, phone, analytics, task management, course building, voice AI — accessible from one dashboard, powered by one AI brain that learns your business.

### What 0nCore Does

**Unified Intelligence Layer**
0nCore adds an AI layer on top of your CRM that no other tool provides. It learns your brand voice, your products, your pricing, your team — and uses that knowledge across every feature. Write an email? It matches your tone. Generate a social post? It knows your hashtags. Answer a phone call? It knows your hours, your services, your FAQ.

**15 AI Knowledge Layers Per Location**
Every location gets 15 dedicated AI knowledge bases:
- **System AI** — knows how to use every feature (always included)
- **Brand AI** — learns your colors, fonts, voice, messaging (auto-populated from Brand Board)
- **Company AI** — your products, services, pricing, FAQs (one free layer included)
- **Personal AI** — adapts to each user's workflow, writing style, preferences
- **12 Custom Layers** — train AI for any purpose: sales scripts, support docs, industry knowledge

**Real-Time Sync Across Everything**
Create a task in 0nCore → it appears in Slack, your calendar, your CRM pipeline. Update a contact → every connected tool reflects the change instantly. Not a 15-minute sync. Not a webhook delay. Real-time, bi-directional, always current.

### Features

**AI Email Builder**
Drag-and-drop email editor with AI content generation. Describe what you want → AI writes the email in your brand voice → send or schedule. Connects to Canva for header images, Figma for design imports, Gamma for layout generation.

**Bulk Social Publisher**
Generate 50+ social media posts from a list of topics. AI creates varied angles — hooks, questions, hot takes, tips, stats — and schedules them across all connected platforms. CSV import/export for mass content management.

**CRO9 Conversion Engine**
Automated conversion rate optimization. Connects to Google Analytics and Search Console. Scores your site across 6 dimensions (traffic, engagement, bounce rate, conversion, SEO, speed). Generates prioritized recommendations with projected impact.

**Development Health Scanner**
Evaluate any website's technical stack, security posture, performance, and code quality. Produces professional PDF reports with actionable recommendations. Three tiers: Stack Health ($18), Security Add-on ($9), Complete Bundle ($25).

**AI Task Management**
Kanban board with personal AI assistant. AutoPlan generates your daily schedule based on priorities and calendar. Focus Mode for deep work sessions with timer and subtask tracking. Command palette (Cmd+K) for instant access to any action.

**Voice AI Agents**
Deploy AI phone agents that answer calls using your knowledge base. Books appointments, takes messages, handles FAQs — sounds human. Configurable with custom greetings, transfer rules, and voicemail.

**AI Course Builder**
Describe a course topic → AI generates the full curriculum: modules, lessons, quizzes, assignments, certificates. Import directly into your CRM course portal with one click.

**Phone System**
Search and purchase phone numbers. Configure forwarding, voicemail, call recording. Track call analytics. Manage across all locations.

**Pipeline & Contact Management**
Full CRM integration with drag-and-drop pipeline, contact management, segmentation, bulk operations, and AI-powered lead scoring.

**Calendar & Booking**
Sync with Google Calendar, CRM calendar, and booking widgets. AI suggests optimal meeting times based on both parties' availability.

**Knowledge Base Manager**
Create, manage, and train up to 15 AI knowledge bases per location. Upload documents, paste URLs, or describe your business — the AI ingests and learns everything.

**Snapshot Manager**
Clone and deploy entire CRM configurations. Pipelines, workflows, custom fields, automations — package once, deploy everywhere.
