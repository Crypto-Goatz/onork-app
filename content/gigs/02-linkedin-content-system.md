## The 6 Workflows (What Runs)

### 1. Daily Post Generator
Fires Tue/Wed/Thu 6am. Generates a VPIS-scored post draft. Routes to your approval queue with score breakdown (Hook, ICP, Pull, Intent, Signal, Velocity, Specificity, Pattern Stack).

### 2. Comment Sniper
You paste a target post URL into a CRM form. AI scores the post (timing, ICP match), generates an engineered comment, routes to your approval queue. You click approve, comment posts.

### 3. Content Publisher
When you approve a post or comment, this workflow handles posting through CRM Social Planner.

### 4. Engagement Tracker
24h after publish, fetches actual engagement (likes, comments, shares, impressions), calculates actual VPIS score, compares to predicted. Feeds the delta back into the formula.

### 5. Reply → DM Pipeline
When a target post author replies to your comment, generates a personalized DM draft. (You send manually — we don't auto-send DMs because LinkedIn detects that.)

### 6. Weekly Intelligence Report
Every Monday 7am, posts a Slack summary: posts published, comments dropped, avg actual VPIS, top patterns, bottom patterns, formula version, prediction accuracy.

**Q: How accurate is VPIS?**

**Q: Can I use this for clients?**
