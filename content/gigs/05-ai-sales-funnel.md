## The Problem

Every business has the same broken funnel:
1. Run ads / post on LinkedIn / send cold emails
2. People click through to a website
3. They fill out a form
4. The form notification lands in a noisy inbox
5. You eventually respond, often days later
6. Most leads went cold by then
7. Of the ones that respond, half are unqualified
8. You waste 30 minutes on each discovery call learning that

The fix:
- **Capture** — page that converts, not just looks pretty
- **Qualify** — AI reads the form responses, scores fit, decides what happens next
- **Route** — hot ICPs go straight to your calendar, others get nurtured by email
- **Nurture** — 5 emails AI-personalized to their specific use case
- **Track** — CRM pipeline shows where every lead is

We've built this 30+ times for clients. We know what works.
